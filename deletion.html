Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Introduction

This Data Deletion Policy explains how users can request the removal of their personal data from our Instagram bot's systems. We respect your right to control your personal information and make the deletion process straightforward.

What Data Can Be Deleted

You can request deletion of the following data:

  • Your Instagram username
  • All messages you have sent to the bot
  • Interaction history and timestamps
  • Any other personal information stored by the bot

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, you can:

  1. Send a direct message to the bot with the text "Delete my data"
  2. Contact us through our support channels with your deletion request
  3. Submit a data deletion request through Meta's Business Suite
Important: Please include your Instagram username in your deletion request to ensure we can properly identify and remove your data.

Deletion Timeline

Once we receive your data deletion request:

  • We will confirm receipt of your request within 48 hours
  • Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete

What Happens After Deletion

After your data is deleted:

  • All personal information will be permanently removed from our systems
  • You will no longer appear in our records
  • The bot will treat you as a new user if you interact with it again
  • Anonymized data used for analytics may be retained, but cannot be linked to you

Exceptions

We may retain certain data if:

  • Required by law or legal proceedings
  • Necessary to prevent fraud or abuse
  • Needed to enforce our terms of service

In such cases, we will retain only the minimum necessary data and delete it as soon as the requirement no longer applies.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Data Deletion Policy or need assistance with your deletion request, please contact us through our support channels.
